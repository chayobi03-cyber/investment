# US Layer — Stress Convergence Assessment

**As of:** 2026-09-02 KST  
**Purpose:** Extend Stress Convergence v0.2 with a US-market layer and define measurable inputs for later threshold/backtest work.

## Executive judgment

**Current US regime: YELLOW / WATCH, not RED.**

The important feature is not broad credit stress yet. It is a **rates + inflation + AI-financing convergence** while conventional equity volatility and HY spreads remain relatively calm. That creates a potentially unstable configuration: the equity market can continue to absorb higher rates while earnings remain strong, but the downside convexity increases if long-end yields rise further or AI capex financing begins to impair credit/earnings expectations.

The US layer therefore should not use VIX or HY spreads alone as the trigger. The primary early-warning combination should be:

> **Long-end Treasury pressure + sticky inflation/energy + weakening labor momentum + rising AI/corporate debt supply**

with credit-spread widening acting as a confirmation variable rather than the initial trigger.

## 1. Current macro/rates state

### Fed

At the July 29, 2026 FOMC meeting, the Fed kept the federal funds target range at **3.50%–3.75%**. The statement said economic activity was expanding at a solid pace, productivity and capital investment were strong, unemployment had changed little, and inflation remained elevated relative to the 2% goal. Three members preferred a 25 bp hike.

Source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260729a.htm

### Inflation

The latest available CPI is July 2026 because the August CPI release is scheduled for **September 11, 2026**. July CPI rose **0.1% m/m and 3.4% y/y**; core CPI rose **0.2% m/m and 2.5% y/y**.

Source: https://www.bls.gov/cpi/news.htm

This means the inflation signal is still above target but not yet an inflation-breakout signal by itself.

### Labor

July 2026 nonfarm payroll employment fell **23,000**, unemployment was **4.1%**, and prior May/June employment gains were revised down by a combined **103,000**. The August Employment Situation is scheduled for **September 4, 2026**.

Source: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.htm

The labor signal therefore deserves more weight than the headline unemployment rate alone suggests: the direction of revisions and payroll momentum are deteriorating.

### Treasury curve

On September 1, 2026, the Treasury curve showed approximately **4.18% at 10Y** and **5.27% at 30Y**. The long end has been the more important stress signal, with 30Y around the highest levels seen in the current regime.

Source: https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?field_tdr_date_value=2026&type=daily_treasury_yield_curve

Recent reporting attributes the long-end pressure to a combination of large fiscal supply, persistent inflation risk, changes in the buyer base/term premium, and heavy corporate borrowing linked to AI infrastructure.

Source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/no-shortage-of-culprits-in-panic-over-long-us-treasury-yields-2026-09-02/

## 2. Credit / liquidity state

### High yield

ICE BofA US High Yield OAS was **2.63% on August 31, 2026**, down from **2.85% on July 31**. This is not a classic broad credit-stress regime.

Source: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/BAMLH0A0HYM2

### Volatility

The VIX closed at **14.51 on August 27, 2026**. Equity volatility is therefore also not confirming systemic stress.

Source: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/VIXCLS/

### Interpretation

Current evidence argues against declaring a generalized US financial-stress event. The warning is earlier in the transmission chain: **rates/funding conditions are tightening before broad credit spreads have materially broken out.**

## 3. AI capex / corporate-financing channel

This is the most important structural addition to the US layer.

NVIDIA's Q2 FY2027 results showed **$96.2B revenue (+106% y/y)** and **$89.0B data-center revenue (+117% y/y)**, confirming that the physical AI buildout is still producing exceptional top-line demand.

Source: https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx

At the same time, AI financing is moving beyond internal cash generation. Reuters reported that AI-related debt issuance reached roughly **$220B in 2026**, with investor demand beginning to show fatigue as large technology issuers increase supply and concessions. Tech bond spreads have widened relative to the overall investment-grade market.

Source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/us-corporate-ai-debt-surge-tests-investor-limits-fatigue-emerges-2026-08-21/

August 2026 US investment-grade corporate borrowing reached a record **$164B**, with AI investment cited as a major driver.

Source: https://www.marketwatch.com/livecoverage/stock-market-today-dow-s-p-500-nasdaq-investors-weighed-escalation-iran-us-manufacturing-pmi-august/card/corporate-debt-borrowing-hit-a-record-in-august-what-that-means-for-september--7H37szeGDQnU9PG31mPm

The key risk mechanism is therefore:

**AI capex → larger corporate debt supply → higher funding demand → long-end Treasury pressure / wider tech credit → higher hurdle rates → multiple compression or lower incremental AI ROI → equity repricing.**

This is a falsifiable channel, not a claim that the AI boom is already over.

## 5. Provisional US trigger architecture

### Level 0 — Normal / risk-on

No convergence. HY spreads stable, labor stable, inflation easing, long yields stable.

### Level 1 — Watch

Any **2** of the following occur within a rolling 20-trading-day window:

1. 10Y yield rises materially while inflation expectations do not improve.
2. Unemployment rises >= 0.3pp from its local low OR 3-month payroll trend turns negative.
3. AI/mega-cap credit spreads widen materially relative to IG.
4. HY OAS rises >= 75 bp from a local low.
5. VIX sustains >20 for 5 trading days.

### Level 2 — Stress convergence

Any **3** of the above, with at least **one rates/inflation signal and one credit/labor signal**.

### Level 3 — Crisis confirmation

Broad credit + labor + equity volatility confirm simultaneously, e.g.:

- HY OAS +150 bp or more from local low;
- VIX >30;
- unemployment acceleration / payroll contraction;
- long-end yields remain elevated despite weakening growth (fiscal/term-premium shock).

These numerical values are **provisional hypotheses**, not validated thresholds. They must be backtested against historical US stress windows before being promoted into v0.2.

## 6. Falsifiers

The current bearish convergence hypothesis is falsified or materially weakened if:

- inflation continues toward 2% without labor deterioration;
- long Treasury yields fall despite large fiscal/corporate supply;
- AI capex converts into sustained free-cash-flow and productivity gains without credit deterioration;
- AI-related corporate issuance is absorbed without spread widening;
- HY spreads remain contained while earnings revisions continue upward.

Conversely, the hypothesis strengthens if **long-end yields rise + AI credit spreads widen + labor weakens + earnings revisions roll over**.

## 7. Link to Korea Layer / global transmission

The US layer should feed the Korea layer through:

**US long yields → global discount rate → USD/KRW → foreign equity flows → KOSPI valuation**

and

**US credit stress → global risk-off → semiconductor/AI capex expectations → Korea exporters → KOSPI earnings expectations.**

Therefore, a US Level-2 trigger should be treated as a higher-priority input to the KOSPI entry-threshold backtest, especially when the Korea Layer is already deteriorating.

## 8. Backtest specification to add next

The next quantitative step is to build the US threshold dataset using daily/monthly histories for:

- 10Y Treasury yield
- 30Y Treasury yield
- 2Y–10Y curve
- CPI / core CPI
- unemployment rate
- payroll 3M average
- HY OAS
- IG OAS
- VIX
- S&P 500 drawdown
- AI/mega-cap credit-spread proxy if available
- oil price / energy inflation proxy

Historical test windows should include at minimum **2000, 2008, 2020, 2022**, plus explicit false-positive periods. The objective is the same as Korea Layer: estimate **FP / FN / lead time**, then calibrate a threshold rather than selecting thresholds by intuition.

## Bottom line

The US is **not yet in broad financial stress**, but the early warning architecture is more concerning than VIX/HY spreads imply. The leading variable is the **long end of the Treasury curve**, and the structurally important amplifier is **AI-driven corporate borrowing/capex**. For Stress Convergence v0.2, the US should therefore be treated as a **rates-led early-warning layer with credit confirmation**, not a credit-led trigger.
