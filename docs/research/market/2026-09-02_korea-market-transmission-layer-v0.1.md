# Korea Market Transmission Layer v0.1

Date: 2026-09-02 KST
Status: **RESEARCH / NOT A PRODUCTION SIGNAL**

## 1. Purpose

Extend the Stress Convergence framework with a Korea-market transmission layer. The layer translates global macro/geopolitical stress into Korea-specific equity-market risk through energy, rates, FX and foreign-flow channels.

## 2. Core transmission chain

`Geopolitics → Energy → Inflation → Fed/Rates → USD/KRW → Foreign Flow → KOSPI → Credit`

The Korea layer is downstream of the global Stress Convergence model and must not replace the existing global trigger logic.

## 3. Primary monitoring variables

| Variable | Initial role | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Brent/WTI | Energy shock | Higher oil raises Korea inflation/import-cost risk |
| US 10Y | Global rates | Higher long rates pressure risk assets and valuation |
| USD/KRW | FX transmission | KRW weakness can amplify foreign-investor risk aversion |
| Foreign KOSPI flow | Positioning | Persistent selling is a direct Korea-market confirmation |
| Semiconductor exports | Earnings/fundamental | Key confirmation of Korea earnings-cycle strength |
| Samsung Electronics / SK hynix flow | Sector confirmation | Useful for identifying foreign-flow pressure in the core index engine |
| KOSPI breadth/volatility | Market response | Confirms whether macro stress is propagating into equities |

## 4. Current 2026-09-02 assessment

- Korea's underlying semiconductor/export cycle remains strong.
- The immediate market risk is primarily external: geopolitics, energy prices, US long rates and foreign flows.
- Therefore, a sharp KOSPI drawdown should not automatically be classified as a Korea-specific fundamental deterioration.
- Conversely, simultaneous deterioration in oil, US 10Y, USD/KRW, foreign flow and semiconductor equities should be treated as a stronger transmission signal.

## 5. Initial investment interpretation

### Risk-on confirmation

Require improving or stable energy/rates conditions plus:

1. foreign KOSPI flow stabilizes or turns positive;
2. Samsung Electronics/SK hynix selling pressure diminishes;
3. US 10Y and oil retreat from stress levels;
4. KOSPI reclaims the relevant technical/risk regime.

### Defensive confirmation

Increase defensive posture when multiple Korea transmission variables deteriorate together:

`Energy ↑ + US10Y ↑ + USD/KRW ↑ + Foreign Flow ↓ + Semiconductor ↓`

This is a confirmation pattern, not yet a calibrated quantitative trigger.

## 6. Provisional market zones

For research and staged-entry planning only:

- KOSPI 6,600–6,750: first accumulation/watch zone.
- KOSPI 6,300–6,500: stronger accumulation zone if macro stress is stabilizing.
- KOSPI 6,000–6,300: stress-scenario accumulation zone, contingent on fundamentals and systemic conditions.
- Above 6,800 with improving foreign flow: confirmation/add-on zone rather than blind chase.

These levels are provisional and must be revalidated against current index composition, volatility and macro regime before being used for capital allocation.

## 7. Portfolio implication

Preferred Korea-market hierarchy under the current thesis:

1. Samsung Electronics / SK hynix and semiconductor core;
2. semiconductor equipment/materials/components selectively;
3. defense;
4. power / ESS;
5. financial/value-up.

The framework favors staged deployment and maintaining liquidity while global macro variables remain unstable.

## 8. Falsifiers

Reopen this layer if:

1. strong semiconductor exports fail to translate into earnings/market support;
2. foreign selling persists despite improving global rates/oil;
3. KOSPI weakness proves primarily domestic rather than externally transmitted;
4. the proposed transmission variables do not improve detection of meaningful Korea drawdowns;
5. provisional accumulation zones show no incremental risk-adjusted value in backtesting.

## 9. Required next validation

Build a Korea-specific historical test using:

- KOSPI daily returns/drawdowns;
- foreign net buying/selling;
- USD/KRW;
- Brent/WTI;
- US 10Y;
- semiconductor export growth;

Measure lead time, false positives, false negatives, drawdown capture and marginal contribution. Do not promote these provisional zones to an automated capital-allocation rule before validation.
