# Lessons Learned — Korea Market Analysis

Date: 2026-09-02 KST

## Lesson 1 — Separate fundamentals from transmission risk

Korea's semiconductor/export fundamentals can remain strong while KOSPI falls because of external energy, rates, FX and foreign-flow shocks. Market drawdown should therefore not be equated automatically with domestic fundamental deterioration.

## Lesson 2 — Add Korea-specific confirmation variables

The global Stress Convergence framework should be complemented by a Korea transmission layer using USD/KRW, foreign KOSPI flow and semiconductor-sector behavior.

## Lesson 3 — Avoid unvalidated precision

The provisional KOSPI accumulation zones are research planning levels, not validated trading thresholds. They require historical backtesting before being promoted to an actionable rule.

## Lesson 4 — Prioritize interaction effects

The highest-value signal is likely not any single variable but the joint deterioration of energy, US rates, FX, foreign flow and semiconductor equities.
